Who We Are

Royal Legacy was founded in 2010 by Chef Arif Rahman with a single mission — to bring world-class dining to everyone's table. What started as a small family kitchen has grown into one of the most beloved restaurants in the city.

We believe food is more than nourishment. It is memory, culture, and connection. Every dish we craft is a tribute to that belief.
